# ACT 6 — LATEST THINKING

## Purpose

The homepage needs both:

- Latest YouTube Videos
- Latest Articles

Do not make them feel like two unrelated template sections.

Create one editorial content system called internally **Latest Thinking** while keeping the visible client-approved labels:

- Latest Videos
- Latest Articles

## Layout

### Desktop

Use a split editorial composition:

```text
06 / LATEST

LATEST
VIDEOS

[ large featured video 16:9 ]
Title
Category / date

                       LATEST ARTICLES
                       01 Article title
                       02 Article title
                       03 Article title
```

One dominant video.
Three article headlines.
No 6-card grid.

Below:
- `View all videos`
- `Read all articles`

## YouTube

Do not auto-play embeds in the homepage hero/story sequence.

Prefer:
- optimized thumbnail
- play affordance
- title
- category
- click to YouTube or open video page

Load real iframe only after interaction if embedding.

## Articles

Use:
- title
- category
- optional date
- no long excerpts on homepage

## Motion

Act 5 topic list narrows into content.

Shared motif:
- selected / final topic underline becomes the top rule of the content feature

Entry:
- large video frame clips up
- article rows reveal 20–40ms apart

Readable state becomes fully static.

## Mobile

Order:

1. Latest Videos heading
2. feature video
3. View all videos
4. Latest Articles heading
5. three article rows
6. Read all articles

No horizontal carousel required.
