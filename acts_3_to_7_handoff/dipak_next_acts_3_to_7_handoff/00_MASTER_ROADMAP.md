# Dipak Vishwakarma Homepage — Acts 3 to 7 Roadmap

## Purpose

Act 2 is being finalized. This pack defines the next homepage scenes so design and development can continue without inventing a new visual language every section.

## Confirmed homepage order after Act 2

3. Featured In
4. My Mission
5. What I Talk About
6. Latest YouTube Videos
7. Latest Articles
8. Authority Closers bridge
9. Final CTA

Featured Podcast and Testimonials remain later / not immediate.

## Global rule

Keep the current Dipak visual DNA:

- warm ivory canvas
- near-black typography
- restrained warm gold
- oversized editorial serif
- clean modern sans
- natural-media brush as a secondary motif only
- no card-heavy SaaS layouts
- no glassmorphism
- no generic dark personal-brand section
- no excessive animation

## Motion rule borrowed from Estate Autopilots

Copy the **motion grammar**, not the visual style:

- first scroll reacts immediately
- one visible motif bridges adjacent acts
- no whole-section ghost crossfades
- deconstruct solid elements via clip/translate
- next scene assembles quickly
- then it holds still long enough to read
- deterministic reversible GSAP timeline
- mobile is simplified, not squeezed desktop cinema

## Recommended production order

1. Finish Act 2.
2. Build Act 3 Featured In.
3. Build Act 4 Mission / belief statement shell.
4. Build Act 5 What I Talk About.
5. Build Act 6 Latest Thinking (YouTube + Articles system).
6. Build Act 7 Authority Closers bridge + final CTA.

Do not build all acts into one enormous timeline immediately. Each act should be independently correct before stitching the transitions.
