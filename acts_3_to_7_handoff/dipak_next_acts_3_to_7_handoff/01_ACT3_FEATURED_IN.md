# ACT 3 — FEATURED IN / AUTHORITY PROOF

## Job of this section

Act 2 says who Dipak is.

Act 3 proves that his ideas exist beyond his own website.

Use the verified media/platform set already supplied:

- Medium
- Dailyhunt
- YouTube
- Podcast
- LinkedIn

Do not invent press publications or logos that are not verified.

## Visual direction

This should feel like a **media wall without becoming a logo wall**.

### Desktop

Use a full-width typographic composition:

```text
03 / PRESENCE

FEATURED
IN.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[ M ]  MEDIUM
       ARTICLES / THINKING

[ DH ] DAILYHUNT
        PUBLISHED PRESENCE

[ ▶ ]  YOUTUBE
       VIDEO / EDUCATION

[ ◉ ]  PODCAST
       CONVERSATIONS

[ in ] LINKEDIN
       PROFESSIONAL THOUGHT
```

Do not use five equal rounded cards.

Instead use one large editorial field with five horizontal or vertical “media signatures”.

### Preferred composition

- Left 30–35%: `FEATURED IN.` huge serif
- Right 65–70%: five media rows
- thin neutral separators
- monochrome icons
- platform name strong
- supporting label tiny and restrained

## Icon policy

Use real brand marks where verified:

- Medium
- YouTube
- LinkedIn

For Podcast use a neutral microphone / waveform icon.

For Dailyhunt:
- use official mark only if verified source asset is available
- otherwise use a typographic monogram / text-only item
- never fabricate a fake brand logo

All marks should render in `currentColor`, monochrome.

## Interaction

Fine pointer only:
- hovering a media row shifts label by 4–6px
- icon scale 1 → 1.04
- separator opacity increases slightly

No bouncing icons.
No brand colors.

## Act 2 → Act 3 transition

Shared object: **Featured In rail** already introduced at the bottom of Act 2.

The Act 2 bottom rail should not disappear.

Instead:

1. rail rises from bottom
2. spacing between platform names expands
3. platform labels become the larger Act 3 media rows
4. `FEATURED IN` label moves into the Act 3 left title position

This is the cleanest possible motif continuity because the next section literally grows from content already visible in Act 2.

## Readable hold

Once Act 3 is assembled, stop major motion.

Target:
- 55–70% of act duration is readable hold
- only subtle pointer response remains
