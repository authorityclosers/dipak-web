## Visual language

Act 4 should feel like a **manifesto page**:

- huge whitespace
- one 2–4 line statement
- one small mission paragraph
- one gold rule
- possibly one extremely faint natural-media brush fragment
- no icons
- no stats
- no cards

## Act 3 → Act 4 transition

Shared motif: **media separators collapse into one gold vertical/horizontal rule**.

Suggested:

1. Act 3 rows compress
2. five separator lines converge into one line
3. platform labels clip away
4. that single line becomes the Act 4 manifesto rule
5. mission typography reveals from behind it

This is analogous to EA’s shared-object transitions but remains editorial.
