# Act 2 — Visual Design Specification

## Static end-state frame

### Overall

- 100svh / 100dvh editorial stage on desktop during the pinned act.
- Same warm ivory family as the hero.
- No card grid.
- No rounded containers.
- No fake dashboard chrome.
- No extra brush decoration simply to fill space.
- Strong black + ivory + warm gold system continues.

### Desktop composition

Use an asymmetric editorial spread:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 02 / THE PERSON                                  THE CERTAINTY      │
│                                                   BUILDER™.          │
│                                                                    │
│ ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────   │
│                                                                    │
│ Founder of Authority Closers.              With over 11 years...   │
│                                            ...implement immediately.│
│                                                                    │
│                                                                    │
│ 11+                         ₹9+ CRORE                    940+        │
│ YEARS                       REVENUE                      TRAINED      │
│                                                                    │
│ ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────   │
│                                            Featured In → preview   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Hierarchy

1. **THE CERTAINTY BUILDER™.** — large editorial serif, ~clamp(4.5rem, 8vw, 9rem), line-height 0.88–0.94.
2. Bio paragraph — restrained sans, 18–21px desktop, max 32–36em.
3. Three proof figures — large serif numbers with tiny sans labels.
4. `Founder of Authority Closers.` — small but prominent identity line.
5. `Featured In` preview only at the bottom edge.

### Stats

Use only the facts already present in the supplied bio:

- `11+` — YEARS OF EXPERIENCE
- `₹9+ CRORE` — REVENUE GENERATED
- `940+` — SALES PROFESSIONALS TRAINED

Do not animate numbers as casino counters. Reveal the actual values as typography.

### Photography

Do **not** force a second large portrait into this section. The hero is already image-heavy.

Act 2 should deliberately become **type-heavy and evidence-heavy**. This creates visual rhythm and avoids the common personal-brand pattern of repeating a portrait in every section.

A small cropped Dipak photo may enter later in the Featured In section, but is not required here.

### Ensō

Use the hero Ensō only as a subtle continuity layer during transition.

At the Act 2 readable hold, it should be almost gone or exist as a very low-opacity crop at one edge. Act 2's dominant geometry is the long gold rule and typography, not the brush circle.

### Featured In preview

At the very bottom / last 10–15% of the Act 2 stage, show only:

`MEDIUM · DAILYHUNT · YOUTUBE · PODCAST · LINKEDIN`

as a low-contrast typographic rail, not logos yet. The next real section can handle verified logos / links / media proof.
