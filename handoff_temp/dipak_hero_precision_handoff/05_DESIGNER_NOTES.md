# 05 — Designer Handoff

## Current visual mistake

The hero now has too many competing "background solutions":

- left brush
- Ensō
- radial light
- portrait
- warm canvas

One of them is broken (left brush), and one is structurally incomplete (current Ensō).

Do not add more decorative objects to compensate.

The fix is **less, but correct**.

## Final visual language

Use:

- warm ivory canvas
- strong editorial serif headline
- Dipak portrait
- one Ensō brush motif
- restrained gold details
- simple black/outline CTA system

Remove:

- left brush for now
- extra haze/noise
- artificial paper blocks
- random brush additions
- unnecessary cards or containers

## Desktop

The portrait should feel like it physically belongs to the right half of the canvas.

The Ensō should be:

- behind head/shoulders
- slightly asymmetric
- lighter than the portrait
- partially allowed to crop
- never perceived as a separate pasted asset

Do not center a perfect circle behind the head like a saint/meditation icon.

## Mobile

The current blank space is not premium whitespace; it is disconnected whitespace.

Premium negative space still has visual tension.

Target:

```text
quote
  ↓ 25–55px
halo energy + face begins
```

The face should appear much earlier than it does now.

Keep:

- full face
- hands
- torso
- chair cue

Allow:

- lower legs/seat to crop at the viewport bottom

## Typography

Do not shrink the headline until the portrait fits.

Fix the portrait geometry first.

Keep the three-line hierarchy:

```text
Sales Is
The Transfer Of
Certainty.
```

## Motion

The load animation should make the layout feel assembled, not make the user wait.

A calm 1-second sequence is enough.

The Estate Autopilots reference is about:

- immediate response
- sophisticated timing
- continuity
- no dead interaction

It is **not** permission to turn this personal-brand hero into a 3D/WebGL experience.
